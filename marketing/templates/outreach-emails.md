## Best Practices

1. **Personalize** - Mention specific products or themes from their profile
2. **Lead with value** - Offer the free report before mentioning your tool
3. **Don't be pushy** - One follow-up max for DMs, two for emails
4. **Timing** - Send emails Tuesday-Thursday, 9am-11am local time
5. **Track responses** - Update leads.json status after each contact
