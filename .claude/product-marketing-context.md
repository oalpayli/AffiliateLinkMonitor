## What It Does (In Plain English)

Affiliate Link Monitor automatically watches your affiliate links 24/7 and sends you an email alert within 60 seconds when something breaks — a product goes out of stock, a link returns a 404, an Amazon ASIN changes, or a tracking tag expires.

Without this tool, affiliates have no way to know their links are broken. Revenue silently bleeds while they publish new content thinking everything is fine.

**Core detection capabilities:**
- Broken links (404 errors, removed pages, expired domains)
- Out-of-stock product alerts (Amazon "Currently Unavailable")
- Invalid affiliate tag detection (expired Associate accounts, wrong tags)
- ASIN change detection (product replaced or discontinued)
- Redirect chain monitoring (extra hops = lost conversions)
- Pinterest pin destination URL verification
- Linktree link health checking
- Bulk URL import and scan

## The Problem We Solve

Affiliate links break constantly and silently. Nobody knows until they look.

**Key statistics (from Affiliate Link Monitor's own analysis):**
- ~15% of Amazon affiliate links break within 6 months
- ~40% of broken links are caused by product removal/discontinuation
- Affiliates with 10k+ monthly visitors lose an estimated $200–$1,400/month to undetected broken links
- The average time an affiliate discovers a broken link manually: 3–6 weeks

**What causes links to break:**
1. Product discontinued or removed (~40% of cases)
2. Amazon Associates account suspended or expired
3. Product ASIN changes (new version replaces old)
4. Out-of-stock / Currently Unavailable — no commission possible
5. Merchant website goes offline
6. Redirect chain changes (Linktree updates, URL structure changes)
7. Tracking tag becomes invalid
